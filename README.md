# 🎬 My Favorite Movie: Mission: Impossible

![Mission Impossible Poster](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/2/23/Mission_Impossible_Fallout_poster.jpg)

## Overview
**Mission: Impossible** is an American action and spy film series starring **Tom Cruise** as *Ethan Hunt*, a secret agent working for the **Impossible Mission Force (IMF)**.

> "Your mission, should you choose to accept it..."  
> — Iconic line from the series

---

### 🎯 What I Like About It
- Intense and realistic **stunts**  
- Smart and suspenseful **plot twists**  
- Great **soundtrack** and iconic theme  
- Tom Cruise doing his **own dangerous stunts!**

---

### 🧨 My Favorite Installments
1. *Mission: Impossible – Ghost Protocol* (2011)
2. *Mission: Impossible – Fallout* (2018)
